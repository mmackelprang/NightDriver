# Night Driver — brand guide

**Proposed display name:** Night Driver
**Tagline:** *1976, after dark.*

## Why this name

The game's own name — the recreation is faithful, the brand should be too.

## The mark

Roadside posts converging on a black horizon over a sliver of steering wheel: the entire game in five shapes.
